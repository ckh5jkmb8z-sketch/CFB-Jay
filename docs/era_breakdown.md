# College Football Era Breakdown (1970-2026)

## 1970s: The Wishbone Era
- **Dominant Offensive System:** Wishbone, Veer, Power Option
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** 5-2, 4-4
- **Key Players:** Earl Campbell, Lee Roy Selmon, Jim Brown (college)
- **Characteristics:** Triple-option dominance, physical run game, low-scoring affairs
- **Notable Programs:** Oklahoma, Nebraska, Texas, Alabama

## 1980s: Transition & Emergence
- **Dominant Offensive System:** I-Form power, early Spread concepts
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** 5-2, 4-3, early Nickel
- **Key Players:** Barry Sanders, Reggie White, Brian Bosworth, Herschel Walker
- **Characteristics:** Option still strong, run/pass balance emerging, specialized defenses
- **Notable Programs:** Oklahoma, Nebraska, Miami, SMU

## 1990s: The Modern Spread Begins
- **Dominant Offensive System:** Spread, Run & Shoot, Air Raid emergence
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** 4-3, Cover 2, Nickel
- **Key Players:** Desmond Howard, Ty Detmer, Marcus Allen, O.J. Simpson
- **Characteristics:** Spread offense revolution, 4-3 defense standard, high-volume passing
- **Notable Programs:** Washington, Nebraska, Florida, Penn State

## 2000s: The Spread Dominance Era
- **Dominant Offensive System:** Spread, Shotgun, Pro-Style hybrid
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** 3-4, Nickel, Tampa 2
- **Key Players:** Tim Tebow, Reggie Bush, Charlie Ward, Lamar Jackson
- **Characteristics:** Spread now dominant, three-safety packages, RPO emergence
- **Notable Programs:** Florida, USC, Texas, Oklahoma

## 2010s: The Uptempo Revolution
- **Dominant Offensive System:** Uptempo Spread, Read Option, Triple-Option modern
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** Multiple Nickels, Dime, Hybrid LB/S
- **Key Players:** Johnny Manziel, Robert Griffin III, Marcus Mariota, Trent Richardson
- **Characteristics:** Extreme pace increase, option renaissance, exotic sub-packages
- **Notable Programs:** Texas A&M, Baylor, Oregon, Alabama

## 2020s: Modern Hybrid Era
- **Dominant Offensive System:** Spread-run blend, RPO, tempo variation
- **Dominant Defensive Formation:** 2-high/1-high shells, Dime packages, Hybrid positions
- **Key Players:** Jeff Demps (historic), Dri Archer, Josh Cribbs, Charles Tillman profiles
- **Characteristics:** System flexibility, coverage science depth, athletic mismatch exploitation
- **Notable Programs:** Georgia, Alabama, Ohio State, TCU

---

Each era includes representative players from all three phases (Offense, Defense, Special Teams).
