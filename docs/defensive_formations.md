# Defensive Formations Reference

## Formation Classifications by Era

### 1970s-1980s: Classic Era Defenses
- **4-4** - Four linemen, four linebackers, three DBs
- **5-2** - Five linemen, two linebackers, four DBs
- **6-2** - Six linemen, two linebackers, three DBs
- **4-3** - Four linemen, three linebackers, four DBs
- **43 Odd** - Nose tackle on center (odd front)
- **43 Even** - Tackle between center and guard (even front)

### 1980s-1990s: Transition Era
- **5-3** - Five linemen, three linebackers
- **6-1** - Six linemen, one linebacker
- **40** - Four linemen, zero linebackers (cover safety concept)
- **Nickel** - Five linemen + 5 DBs (receiver-heavy situations)
- **Dime** - Four linemen + 6 DBs (long yardage)
- **Quarter** - Four linemen + 7 DBs (extreme passing situations)

### 1990s-2000s: Modern Era
- **3-4** - Three linemen, four linebackers, four DBs
- **34 Base** - Standard 3-4 alignment
- **34 Nickel** - 3-4 with 5th DB instead of LB
- **46 Defense** - Linebacker-heavy blitz package
- **Tampa 2** - Cover 2 safeties, over-the-top middle LB
- **Cover 0** - Man coverage, no deep safeties
- **Cover 1** - Man with 1 deep safety
- **Cover 2** - Two safeties, split middle
- **Cover 3** - Three deep zone (corners + 1 safety)
- **Cover 4** - Quarters coverage (4 deep)

### 2000s-2010s: Contemporary Systems
- **Nickel Lineups** - 5 DBs, 3 LBs, 3 DL
- **Dime Packages** - 6 DBs, 2 LBs, 3 DL
- **Sub-Package Defenses** - LB to DB substitution emphasis
- **Exotic Blitzes** - Secondary-based pressure packages
- **Inverted Safety** - Safety playing linebacker box
- **Hybrid LB/S** - Linebacker-sized safeties

### 2010s-2020s: Modern Hybrid
- **Multiple Nickels** - Various 5-6 DB looks
- **Pressuring Nickel** - Aggressive blitz-oriented sub-packages
- **Big Nickel** - Larger DB (safety/nickel hybrid)
- **Apex Player** - Hybrid position LB/S
- **Cover 0 Looks** - All-out man coverage with blitz
- **2-High Shells** - Two safeties, varied front
- **1-High Shells** - One safety deep, various front looks

## Statistical Tracking by Formation

For each formation:
- **Yards Allowed/Play** - Average opponent yards gained
- **Efficiency Against** - EPA allowed to offense
- **Pressure Rate %** - QB pressure on passing plays
- **Explosion Play % Allowed** - Big plays given up
- **Turnover Margin** - Takeaways in formation
- **Success Rate %** - Stops/short gains gained
- **Conference Dominance** - Win % when formation used
